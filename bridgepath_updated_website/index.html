Karibu BridgePath!

BridgePath Education Initiative Tanzania ni jitihada ya kielimu inayolenga kuwaunganisha wanafunzi, shule, na jamii ya Tanzania na washirika wa kimataifa katika nchi kama Uingereza na Ujerumani.

BridgePath is an educational initiative aiming to connect students, schools, and communities in Tanzania with global partners in countries such as the UK and Germany.
